Problem Statement

Clients looking to hire designers for niche or specialized work — such as garment/textile design, thumbnail design, or motion graphics — struggle to find and vet the right specialist. General freelance platforms (Fiverr, Upwork) group all "graphic designers" together with no category-specific filtering, portfolio depth, or trust signals, making it hard for clients to identify who is actually skilled in the specific niche they need. At the same time, designers — especially newer ones — lack a dedicated community space to share work, get feedback, and build visibility within their specific niche.

Objectives

To design a mobile/web application where clients can search, filter, and hire designers based on specific design niches (e.g., graphic, garment, thumbnail, motion), hourly rate, and reviews.
To create designer profiles with portfolio showcases, ratings, and category tags that help clients evaluate expertise before hiring.
To build a community feed where designers can share their work, get feedback from peers, and grow visibility within the platform.
To develop a booking/hiring flow allowing clients to request and confirm work directly through the app.
To identify and structure sustainable revenue streams for the platform, including commission on bookings, verified-badge subscriptions, and boosted/featured listings.
